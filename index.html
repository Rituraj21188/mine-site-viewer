Advantages of Drone Survey in Mining

High-precision aerial photogrammetry delivers rapid site capture, centimeter-level volumetric accuracy, and eliminates hazardous manual ground surveys.

Fast, Comprehensive Coverage

Survey entire mining leases and quarries rapidly, providing comprehensive, authentic data on operational site conditions without interrupting active pit traffic.

Centimeter-Level Volumetrics

Produces dense 3D point clouds and Digital Surface Models (DSM) for accurate cut-and-fill volumetrics and excavation monitoring.

Irregular Stockpile Modeling

Stockpiles exhibiting complex geometries, steep angles, or irregular craters are surveyed with exact precision compared to traditional methods.

Zero Ground Personnel Hazard

Executes entirely from the air, removing field surveyors from dangerous pit benches, highwalls, active dump slopes, and heavy equipment routes.

Automated Multi-Epoch Tracking

Historical survey datasets overlay seamlessly to automatically track and highlight excavation advancement, dump growth, and backfilling over time.

Perimeter & Buffer Oversight

Enables systematic lease boundary demarcation and captures the mandatory 100-meter buffer zone outside the boundary to identify unauthorized excavation.

Systematic Mine Closure Planning

Creates a multi-year digital spatial archive to monitor environmental rehabilitation, greenbelt afforestation, and scientific mine closure planning.

Digital Audit Database

Establishes an easily retrievable digital repository of raw and processed spatial data preserved for mandatory statutory verification periods.

Execution & Processing Pipeline

Operating strictly according to Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) flight guidelines and IBM SOP guidelines under Rule 34A.

01 — CAPTURE

Nadir Aerial Acquisition

Conducted with calibrated ≥20 MP RGB sensors at vertical nadir (90°) angles, maintaining ≥80% forward and ≥70% lateral overlap during midday overhead sun conditions.

02 — GEOREFERENCE

DGPS Ground Control

Establishment of ≥5 high-contrast GCPs per sq. km with dual-frequency DGPS instruments, tied to permanent undisturbed benchmarks ensuring RMSE < 5 cm.

03 — ANALYZE & SUBMIT

Photogrammetric Outputs

Generation of sub-5 cm orthomosaics, DSM/DTM elevation rasters, thematic polygon shapefiles, and IBM-compliant submission dossiers.
